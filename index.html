The specifics

Premium buyers check the details, so here they are. This is the plan for the first version. Anything marked "To be confirmed" gets filled in as prototype testing finishes.

MaterialDiatomite (diatomaceous earth) stone top for absorption, with a natural cork base to grip and protect furniture. Matte, unsealed finish with a slightly eased edge.
Size4" round, about 0.4" thick
WeightAbout 8 oz per coaster (estimated): a diatomite top, a thin stainless steel plate for heft, and a cork base. Final weight to be confirmed on the prototype.
Glass rangeFits glass bases from about 2" to 3.5": shot, rocks, highball, pint, standard wine and most mugs. Not made for oversized wine or stemless glasses.
CareHand wash only. Wipe with a damp cloth, never submerge, and air dry. For stains, wet the surface and lightly sand it with fine or medium sandpaper to restore absorbency.
ColorsTwo matte neutrals: Warm Stone (light gray-beige) and Charcoal, each with a natural cork base.
Target price$28 per set of 4 (about $7 each). This is a target, not a final price.
